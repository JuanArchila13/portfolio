Curador de Arte Urbano

Technical-artistic analysis platform based on generative AI, computer vision, RAG, and multimodal language models for the automated generation of reports on urban art works.

01 — Description

Urban art analysis with artificial intelligence

I developed Curador de Arte Urbano to explore how artificial intelligence can help us understand an artwork beyond its first impression. The platform analyzes images of urban art and generates reports covering their colors, composition, techniques, and context.

I combined Python and OpenCV for image processing with language models running locally through llama.cpp and Ollama. I chose Qwen3.5 4B with Q4 quantization to balance analysis quality and resource usage, without relying on cloud AI services.

During development, I compared different models and refined their prompts to guide the analysis. I incorporated details extracted from each image, such as its color palette, typography, and condition, to produce more consistent responses focused on the artwork's characteristics.

I used LangChain to connect the language models with the RAG system and build agents with information retrieval tools. These tools can look up information about the characteristics being analyzed, such as color, composition, and typography, and retrieve references from specialist documents. The reports combine visual analysis with information that helps place the artwork in context.

02 — RAG Pipeline

Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Preprocessing

Normalization of PDF content by removing special characters, typographic noise, and irrelevant elements before indexing.

Segmentation

Document splitting into overlapping chunks, preserving context between fragments to maximize information retrieval.

Hybrid selection

Combined evaluation of structural similarity (40%) and semantic similarity through embeddings (60%), with a minimum threshold of 70%.

Indexing

Storage of selected chunks in ChromaDB to build a specialized and updatable vector database.

Agents and information retrieval tools

With LangChain, I connected the agents to tools that retrieve information related to each artwork's characteristics. Top-K retrieval in ChromaDB supplies excerpts from specialist documents to complement the model's analysis. This workflow adds context to the responses and allows references to be updated without retraining the base model.

03 — Architecture

System components

The platform combines multiple artificial intelligence components for the technical-artistic analysis of urban art works:

  • Chromatic analysis engine with Python and OpenCV, using K-Means and CIELAB, HSV, and RGB color spaces to extract color palettes.
  • RAG pipeline with ChromaDB, specialized embeddings, and Top-K retrieval to enrich the model's responses.
  • LangChain orchestration to connect language models, document retrieval, and information lookup tools.
  • Agents with tools to retrieve information about color, composition, typography, and other characteristics analyzed in the artworks.
  • Local LLM execution through Ollama and llama.cpp, evaluating multimodal models according to their capabilities.
  • Prompt Engineering to improve the consistency, quality, and specificity of generated analyses.
