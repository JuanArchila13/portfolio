01 — Description

Sports management for the university community

I participated in the development of software focused on the management of the Physical Conditioning Centers (CAF) of the Universidad Pedagógica y Tecnológica de Colombia (UPTC). The system was designed for user management and student appointment scheduling at the university's physical conditioning centers.

The solution sought to digitize and optimize registration, access control, and physical activity scheduling processes, improving the experience for both students and sports center administrators.

03 — Architecture

Microservices-based system

The system was designed under a microservices architecture that allows each component to scale independently and maintain a clear separation of responsibilities:

  • Microservices in Spring Boot to modularize the system's business logic.
  • REST API as the communication interface between the frontend and backend services.
  • MySQL for structured persistence of users, appointments, and resources.
  • React as the presentation layer for a modern and responsive interface.
  • AWS Lightsail for deployment and hosting of the application.
