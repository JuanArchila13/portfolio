01 — Description

Transportation management with innovation

MueveT is an information system developed within a research project funded by the Colombian Ministry of Science, Technology and Innovation (Minciencias), aimed at strengthening urban public transportation management through the integration of IoT technologies, data analysis, and real-time monitoring.

The platform stands out for its scalable architecture, both in functionality and deployment, implementing a hybrid data persistence system that allows structured and unstructured data to be stored efficiently.

02 — Features

Main features

Operational management

Management of operating companies, vehicles, drivers, and owners under a Multi-Tenant philosophy.

Routes and stops

Management of routes, segments, stops, and operational schedules of the transportation system.

Real-time monitoring

Vehicle tracking through IoT devices and calculation of estimated arrival times (ETA) at stops.

IoT NOC

Network Operations Center to monitor the status and health of deployed IoT devices.

Alerts and infractions

Management of alerts and operational infractions for transportation service control.

Indicators and reports

Visualization of service quality indicators and generation of reports to support decision-making.

Smart stops

Visualization of environmental variables: temperature, humidity, noise, air quality, and solar intensity.

Reports

Report generation for operating companies and mobility authorities.

03 — Architecture

Scalable architecture

MueveT implements architectural styles oriented toward scalability, maintainability, and efficient real-time event processing:

  • Event-Driven Architecture (EDA) for handling real-time events from IoT devices.
  • API-First in the backend, facilitating integration with multiple clients and services.
  • Feature-Based Architecture in the frontend for modular code organization.
  • MQTT as a lightweight communication protocol for IoT devices.
  • Hybrid persistence with MySQL for structured data and MongoDB for unstructured data.
